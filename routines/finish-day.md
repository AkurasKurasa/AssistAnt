Nightly wrap-up for the Notion Tasks page.

1. Reader finds today's block in the synced block, plus open Reminders and Notes.
2. Summarizer writes a warm summary. Struck-through items count as skipped on purpose.
3. Matcher marks finished items Done in Reminders. Weekly House Clean gets a new one for next Saturday.
4. Notes Scout looks for deadlines in Notes that aren't in Reminders. These are guesses, so they need approval.
5. Chore Keeper makes sure every non-daily chore has an open Reminder.
6. Planner builds tomorrow: deadlines due within 3 days, leftovers, three empty blocks, then the weekday routine.
7. Writer makes the safe changes and holds the risky ones (new guessed Reminders, deleting today) for approval.
8. Checker re-reads Notion and sends anything wrong back to Writer.
